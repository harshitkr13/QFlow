# Phase 10 — Patient Queue Tracking

## Goal
Let patients track their queue position and plan when to arrive.

## Display after proceeding
- current token
- own token
- patients ahead
- estimated waiting window
- expected consultation window
- recommended arrival
- doctor status
- applicable break/unavailability information

## Update Strategy
Use HTTP polling while the queue screen is active.

## Acceptance Criteria
Patient sees updated queue information after staff advances the queue.
